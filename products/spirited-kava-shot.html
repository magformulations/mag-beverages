About Spirited Kava Shot

The Spirited Kava Shot offers all the advantages of the Infuser in a convenient single dose — so you can enjoy it without measuring, or try it before committing to the larger bottle. It’s a non-alcoholic drink infuser with great ingredients and more versatility, pre-dosed for you.

Made with real juices and organic coconut water, with no artificial sweeteners. The addition of ashwagandha and L-theanine may help reduce stress and improve focus and attention. The perfect blend of kava and other naturally occurring adaptogens and nootropics, along with electrolytes, may help you improve hydration, support mental health, and magnify the good times.

How to serve

The Shot is a complete dose on its own — toss it back and go. When you want a drink in your hand, it doubles as a ready-made functional mixer. Three to start:

No. 1

The Kava Mule

The best place to start

Ingredients
  • 1 Spirited Kava Shot (2 oz)
  • 4 oz spicy ginger beer
  • Juice of ½ lime
  • Ice, plus a lime wheel
Method

Build over ice in a mug: Shot, lime juice, then ginger beer. Stir once and garnish.

No. 2

Sparkling Citrus + Sea Salt

The minimalist build

Ingredients
  • 1 Spirited Kava Shot (2 oz)
  • 5 oz quality sparkling water
  • Juice of an orange & lime wedge
  • A pinch of flaky sea salt
  • Ice
Method

Pour the Shot over ice, squeeze in the citrus, add the salt, and top with sparkling water. Serve right away.

No. 3

The Wellness Hour

Wind-down without clocking out

Ingredients
  • 1 Spirited Kava Shot (2 oz)
  • 4 oz chilled coconut water
  • ½ oz lavender simple syrup
  • Juice of ¼ lemon
  • Ice
Method

Stir the Shot, coconut water, lavender syrup and lemon over ice. Garnish with a strip of lemon peel.
